# Entertainer starter entries

These nine records seed [the listing file](../src/entertainers/listings.json) with public professional names and examples for all six predefined categories. They are project-added examples, not applications from the named people or studios. All start as `pending`, with empty payment fields and no expiry. They do not highlight, and no endorsement, participation, holding, or payment is claimed.

Categories describe public professional work, including established career history; they are not a claim about current availability or private relationships. The couples category refers to publicly described couple-produced content. Public profiles and articles are research references; maintainers still need to verify control of a creator's accounts before approval. No birth names, private contact details, or wallet addresses have been inferred.

## Turning a starter record into a submitted listing

The creator or authorized representative should update the existing record rather than add a duplicate, confirm their categories, and supply creator-controlled public profile links. Follow the [README submission and payment instructions](../README.md#for-entertainers-and-creators-get-listed). Maintainers set approval and expiry only after verification. These starter records do not bypass the unconfigured payment policy.
